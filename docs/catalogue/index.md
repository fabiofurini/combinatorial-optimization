# Catalogue of combinatorial problems

All the problems defined in the course, by family. Each problem has its own page: its definition, its assumptions and what the course does on it, technique by technique.

[:material-file-pdf-box: Catalogue (PDF)](../pdf/combinatorial-problem-catalogue.pdf){ .md-button } [:material-table: The problems at a glance](../combinatorial-optimization-problems/problems.md){ .md-button }

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Knapsack problems**

    ---

    Select a subset or a multiset of items of maximum total profit subject to capacity constraints.

    [0/1 knapsack (KP)](knapsack-problem.md) · [Subset sum (SSP)](subset-sum-problem.md) · [Bounded knapsack (BKP)](bounded-knapsack-problem.md) · [Unbounded knapsack (UKP)](unbounded-knapsack-problem.md) · [Multidimensional knapsack (MDKP)](multidimensional-knapsack-problem.md) · [Multiple knapsack (MKP)](multiple-knapsack-problem.md) · [Multiple-choice knapsack (MCKP)](multiple-choice-knapsack-problem.md) · [Knapsack with setups (KPS)](knapsack-problem-with-setups.md)

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The family](knapsack-problems.md)

-   **Packing problems**

    ---

    Pack items into the minimum number of bins of equal capacity.

    [Bin packing (BPP)](bin-packing-problem.md) · [Cutting stock (CSP)](cutting-stock-problem.md)

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The family](packing-problems.md)

-   **Coloring problems**

    ---

    Color the vertices of a graph with the minimum number of colors.

    [Vertex coloring (VCP)](vertex-coloring-problem.md)

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The family](coloring-problems.md)

-   **Clique problems**

    ---

    Find a set of pairwise adjacent vertices of maximum cardinality or weight.

    [Maximum clique (MCP)](maximum-clique-problem.md) · [Maximum stable set (MSSP)](maximum-stable-set-problem.md) · [Vertex-weighted maximum clique (VWMCP)](vertex-weighted-maximum-clique-problem.md) · [Edge-weighted maximum clique (EWMCP)](edge-weighted-maximum-clique-problem.md)

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The family](clique-problems.md)

-   **Routing problems**

    ---

    Find a shortest tour that visits every city exactly once.

    [Travelling salesman (TSP)](travelling-salesman-problem.md) · [Metric TSP](metric-travelling-salesman-problem.md) · [Asymmetric TSP (ATSP)](asymmetric-travelling-salesman-problem.md) · [Hamiltonian cycle (HCP)](hamiltonian-cycle-problem.md)

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The family](routing-problems.md)

-   **Scheduling problems**

    ---

    Assign jobs to parallel machines so as to minimize the makespan.

    [Identical parallel machines (P||Cmax)](scheduling-on-identical-parallel-machines.md) · [Fixed number of identical machines (Pm||Cmax)](scheduling-on-a-fixed-number-of-identical-machines.md) · [Parallel machines with different speeds (Q||Cmax)](scheduling-on-parallel-machines-with-different-speeds.md) · [Unrelated parallel machines (R||Cmax)](scheduling-on-unrelated-parallel-machines.md)

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The family](scheduling-problems.md)

-   **Lot sizing problems**

    ---

    Plan the production over a horizon of periods at minimum cost.

    [Capacitated lot sizing (CLS)](capacitated-lot-sizing.md) · [Uncapacitated lot sizing (ULS)](uncapacitated-lot-sizing-problem.md) · [Capacitated lot sizing with constant capacity (CLS-C)](capacitated-lot-sizing-with-constant-capacity.md) · [Lot sizing with several products and setup times (MLS)](lot-sizing-with-several-products-and-setup-times.md) · [Lot sizing with initial stock (LS-SI)](lot-sizing-with-initial-stock.md) · [Lot sizing with overtime (LS-OT)](lot-sizing-with-overtime.md) · [Lot sizing with unsatisfied demand (LS-UD)](lot-sizing-with-unsatisfied-demand.md)

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The family](lot-sizing-problems.md)

-   **Miscellaneous problems**

    ---

    Problems with an exact total length or amount, solved by dynamic programming.

    [Rod cutting (RCP)](rod-cutting-problem.md) · [Coin change (CCP)](coin-change-problem.md)

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The family](miscellaneous-problems.md)

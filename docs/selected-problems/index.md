# Selected problems

The problems on which the whole course focuses, introduced one by one: definition, assumptions, variants and a first example.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **The knapsack problem**

    ---

    Choose a subset of items of maximum total profit whose total weight does not exceed the capacity.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The problem](../combinatorial-optimization-problems/knapsack/index.md)

-   **The bin packing problem**

    ---

    Pack items of given weights into the minimum number of identical bins of capacity $c$.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The problem](../combinatorial-optimization-problems/bin-packing/index.md)

-   **The vertex coloring problem**

    ---

    Color the vertices of a graph so that adjacent vertices get different colors, using the minimum number of colors.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The problem](../combinatorial-optimization-problems/coloring.md)

-   **The maximum clique problem**

    ---

    Find a largest set of pairwise adjacent vertices of a graph.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The problem](../combinatorial-optimization-problems/clique/index.md)

-   **The travelling salesman problem**

    ---

    Find a shortest closed route that visits every city exactly once and returns to the starting city.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The problem](../combinatorial-optimization-problems/tsp/index.md)

-   **Scheduling on identical parallel machines**

    ---

    Assign jobs to $m$ identical machines so that the makespan, the largest machine load, is minimum.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The problem](../combinatorial-optimization-problems/pcmax.md)

-   **Capacitated lot sizing**

    ---

    Plan the production of every period, with capacities, setup costs and stock, at minimum total cost.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The problem](../combinatorial-optimization-problems/lot-sizing/index.md)

</div>
